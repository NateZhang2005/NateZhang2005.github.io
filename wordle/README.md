Welcome to my first coding project: a customizable wordle simulator! Challenge your friends with inside jokes, slang, or anything else that wouldn't normally appear on wordle. You can also give your friends more guesses if you're feeling nice or less, if you're feeling evil. Additional instructions are below:

Upon running, you will directed to the main frontpage, which simply has the "create" button. The button will direct you to a form that asks you for a word, a number of guesses you would like your friend to have, and whether or not you want your word (as well as all following guesses) to be from a wordle database. This database contains all possible wordle guesses.

If you choose that you don't want a real wordle word, your word can be anything and of any length. A corresponding wordle game will be created
to match your inputs. As a result, the guesses also do not have to be a real word (yes, you can literally type anything and it'll check your
guess)

If you choose that you DO want a real word, your input word has to be 5 characters long and has to be contains withined the possible guesses
database. As a result, all of the guesses have to also be contained within the database.

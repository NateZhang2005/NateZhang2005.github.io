Upon running, you will directed to the main frontpage, which simply has the "create" button. The button will direct you to a form that asks you
for a word, a number of guesses you would like your friend to have, and whether or not you want your word (as well as all following guesses) to
be from a wordle database. This database contains all possible wordle guesses.

If you choose that you don't want a real wordle word, your word can be anything and of any length. A corresponding wordle game will be created
to match your inputs. As a result, the guesses also do not have to be a real word (yes, you can literally type anything and it'll check your
guess)

If you choose that you DO want a real word, your input word has to be 5 characters long and has to be contains withined the possible guesses
database. As a result, all of the guesses have to also be contained within the database.

This page is meant for you to challenge your friends, but I included to have non-real wordle words so you can have fun with inside jokes and such!
I hope you can have a little bit of fun with it!
